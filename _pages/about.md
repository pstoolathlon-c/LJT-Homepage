## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.ust.hk/), supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://web.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/junxianh/). I completed my B.Eng. at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024. My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing** and **Machine Learning**, with particular interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
